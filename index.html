I am an undergraduate researcher in Computer Science at Kookmin University, advised by Prof. Sangmin Yoon in the HCI Lab.

I study how robots can use what they see—together with predictions of what may happen next—to decide how to move. My work spans temporal visual modeling, cooperative 3D perception, and efficient vision-language systems.

My long-term goal is to connect predictive visual representations with planning and active perception, enabling robots to reason about changing environments and navigate autonomously. Previously, I was a visiting undergraduate researcher in the Dutt Research Group at UC Irvine, advised by Prof. Nikil Dutt.
